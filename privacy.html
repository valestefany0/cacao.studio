Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

Who is responsible for your data

Valeria Ocaña (Cacao Studio) is responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach me at valeriae.ocana@gmail.com. I am based in Spain.

What I collect and why

  • When you book a call. Your name, email, and anything you write in the booking form. I use it to schedule and hold the call. Booking is handled by Calendly.
  • When you email me. Your email address and what you write, so I can answer you.
  • When we work together. The details you share about your business, and billing details needed to invoice and get paid.

I use this information to answer you, to run calls and sessions, and to meet my legal and tax duties. I do not sell your data, and I do not use it for advertising.

On what legal basis

To answer your request and to provide a service you ask for (steps before and during a contract). To meet legal obligations, such as keeping invoices. And, where it applies, my legitimate interest in running my business and replying to people who contact me.

Who else sees it

I use a few services to run my business. They process data on my behalf and have their own privacy policies:

  • Calendly, for bookings.
  • Google (Gmail), for email.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website and may keep basic technical logs, such as your IP address, when you visit.
  • Payment providers, when you pay for a session or a service.

Some of these companies are based outside the European Union, mainly in the United States. In those cases, the transfer relies on the safeguards these companies provide under European data protection rules.

Cookies and fonts

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. The page fonts and styling are loaded from Google Fonts and a content delivery network, so those services can see your IP address when a page loads. When you open the Calendly booking page, Calendly applies its own cookies and policy.

How long I keep it

I keep messages and booking details for as long as needed to deal with your request and any follow up. Invoices and accounting records are kept for the period Spanish law requires. After that, I delete the data.

Your rights

You can ask me to give you access to your data, correct it, delete it, limit how I use it, or send it to you in a portable format. You can also object to how I use it. Write to valeriae.ocana@gmail.com and I will answer. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es), or to the authority in your own country.

Changes

If I change how I handle data, I will update this page and the date at the top.
