About

Hi, I am Valeria.

I studied international business and quality management, and I have spent about eight years working with brands, creators, and teams. I have led teams, built content systems, and run campaigns, so I know what it takes to get things finished, not just planned.

Most owners I meet are very good at what they do and never trained in marketing or admin. They have plenty of ideas, no clear order for them, and nobody to keep things moving. That is the part I help with.

I do not think you have to compete with the big brands. I care about brands that stay true to themselves, and I want to help you grow in a way that still feels like yours.

You bring the heart. I bring the structure.

I work in English and Spanish, with clients wherever they are.
