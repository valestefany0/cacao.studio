Frequently Asked Questions

Who do you work with?

Owners of brands with a mission, in wellness, fashion, sustainability, and beyond. They already run a business with paying customers, are ready to invest in growing it, and never trained in marketing or admin.

Do I need a plan before we start?

No. Starting without one is exactly what the Strategic Session is for. Bring the messy list.

Will you find the people for me?

I check whether you need the hire, define the role, run the process, and shortlist candidates. If someone in my network fits, I will introduce you. I do not promise a ready made team.

Will you do the work yourself?

Yes, in the areas I know: strategy, social media, copywriting, email and basic automation, and influencer campaigns. If something needs a specialist, such as a designer or advanced automation, I bring one in. Their fees are separate and quoted before anything starts.

Is Coaching and Strategic Support full time?

No. It is part time: a set amount of my time each month, agreed up front. I take on a small number of clients at a time so each one gets proper attention.

Is Coaching a project or a monthly retainer?

Either one. Some owners need a single project finished, and others need ongoing support. We decide on the call, and we can talk about retainers there. Projects can be as short as a month, and for ongoing work I suggest at least 3 months.

How do you handle budget?

We talk about it in the short free call, so the plan fits what you can actually invest.

Do you work remotely?

Yes, with clients across different markets and time zones, in English and Spanish.
